Questions

Is the computer’s choice really random?

In Random mode, yes: it picks one of three moves with equal chance each round. Adaptive mode adds a little strategy on top.

Can I use the keyboard?

Yes. Press R, P or S to play rock, paper or scissors.

Why can’t I click during a round?

Buttons pause for a moment during the countdown so a round finishes before the next begins.
